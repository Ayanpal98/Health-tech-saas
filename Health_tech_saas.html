How it works

From request to care in three steps

  1. 1

    Patient sends a request

    They pick the specialty they need, add a short note, and set how far they can travel. Location is shared only for matching.

  2. 2

    HealthSync finds the best match

    The geo-intelligence layer ranks available verified providers by specialty, service area, and distance, then notifies the top match.

  3. 3

    Provider connects and follows up

    The consultant accepts, consults, and sends any prescription to a nearby pharmacy. The patient tracks it all in one place.

FAQ

Questions we hear most

Does AI make medical decisions?

No. AI sorts requests, suggests provider matches, and sends notifications. Diagnosis and treatment always stay with licensed medical professionals.

How are providers and pharmacies verified?

Each provider submits registration and licence details, which our team reviews before their profile goes live. Verification status is shown on every profile.

How is patient location used?

Location is used to rank nearby providers and pharmacies for a request. Patients control what they share, and access is limited by role and recorded in an audit trail.

What does the free pilot include?

A full setup for one location with all portals for your plan, guided onboarding, and support while you test with real staff. You decide whether to go live afterwards.

Can it connect to our existing systems?

Integration with existing hospital and pharmacy systems is available on the State & Enterprise plan. Tell us what you use and we will scope it with you.

Can we start in one district and expand later?

Yes. HealthSync is built geo-first, so you can add districts, cities, or states without changing how providers and patients use it.
